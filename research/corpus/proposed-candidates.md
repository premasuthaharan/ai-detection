Nothing here has been fetched into the corpus, snapshotted, or measured. Tick
the boxes you approve (or delete lines). Only approved entries get added to
`manifest.json`; the dev/holdout split is derived from the final approved set
(see `research/README.md`), so it can't be chosen from this list.

Notes on how these were found: the AI candidates come from public vendor
galleries (read for URLs only). The human candidates come from my own
knowledge plus an Awwwards search, so their provenance still has to be
verified (see "Verification before adding").

## Inclusion criteria

- **AI:** a vendor or creator claim that the site was generated by an AI
  builder. Prefer organization or vendor-owned pages over private individuals'
  sites, because manifest URLs and reports are committed to a public repo.
- **Human:** launched or last redesigned before Nov 2022 (pre-ChatGPT) with a
  documented human design team, and **modern and polished**, so it is a hard
  negative (it uses gradients, rounded cards, shadows, popular fonts, etc.).

## Verification before adding

For every approved human site, before it enters the manifest:

1. Check Internet Archive capture dates to confirm the design predates Nov 2022
   (or that the current design is a known human-team redesign).
2. Record the design credit (team or studio) as the manifest `claim`.
3. Mark `kind: "hard-negative"` so reports stop warning about the missing class.

For AI sites: record the gallery page that lists them as the `claim`, and
note if the site is a vendor demo versus a user submission.
