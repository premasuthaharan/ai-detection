# C25 — Generic imperative microcopy

**Reported tell:** "Boost Your Workflow", "Streamline Your Experience", "Unlock Productivity", "Next Generation Platform"; generic copy called "one of the strongest AI signals" (S2).

**Proposed mechanism (hypothesis):** Generated copy is polished and vague: an imperative verb plus a generic noun.

**Measurement:** Count of headings and button/link labels matching a verb + generic-noun pattern, or stock phrases like next-generation. Checked in headings and calls to action rather than body text. The shipping `buzzwords` signal counts word presence in body text and is weighted at 1.

**Known failure modes:** Marketing copy written by humans is full of the same phrases.

**Open questions:** S2 calls this very strong; our weight for `buzzwords` is the lowest in the rubric. The data should settle it.
