# C11 — Eyebrow label above headings

**Reported tell:** "BIG WORDS ABOVE HEADLINES" (S1b). Ambiguous wording; interpreted as a short, small, uppercase or letter-spaced label above a heading. Needs confirmation of intent.

**Proposed mechanism (hypothesis):** Generated sections pair each heading with a small category label above it.

**Measurement:** Count of h1/h2 headings preceded by a short (five words or fewer), much smaller label that is uppercase or letter-spaced.

**Known failure modes:** Editorial and magazine styles; many human-designed marketing sites use eyebrows.

**Open questions:** What S1b meant exactly.
