# C13 — Giant, bold, centered hero h1

**Reported tell:** "Giant bold h1 centered in the hero section" (S1b); "oversized headings" (S2).

**Proposed mechanism (hypothesis):** Generated hero sections center a very large bold headline over supporting copy.

**Measurement:** For the first h1 in the first viewport: number of conditions met out of three (font size 48px or more, weight 700 or more, horizontally centered).

**Known failure modes:** Extremely common on human-designed marketing pages. Likely low precision alone.

**Open questions:** Whether it adds anything once combined with other hero signals.
