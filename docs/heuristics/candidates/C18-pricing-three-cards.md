# C18 — Three-card pricing layout

**Reported tell:** "3-card pricing layouts" (S2).

**Proposed mechanism (hypothesis):** Generated pricing sections default to three tiers side by side with the middle one highlighted.

**Measurement:** Largest group of sibling cards that each contain a price pattern, a billing period and a button or link.

**Known failure modes:** Three-tier pricing is the dominant human SaaS pattern too.

**Open questions:** Likely high prevalence in both classes.
