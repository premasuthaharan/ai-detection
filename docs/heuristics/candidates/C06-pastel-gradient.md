# C06 — Pastel gradients

**Reported tell:** "pastel gradients for all other types of products" (S2).

**Proposed mechanism (hypothesis):** For non-tech products generators switch to soft, light gradients instead of neon ones.

**Measurement:** Count of elements (at least 40x40px) with a gradient whose stops are light and still colored.

**Known failure modes:** Soft gradients are a common human aesthetic (wellness, lifestyle brands).

**Open questions:** Only one source. May not discriminate at all.
