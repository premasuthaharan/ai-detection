# C27 — Icons or emoji that do not match what they represent

**Reported tell:** "Icons and emojis that don't match what they are representing" (S1a).

**Proposed mechanism (hypothesis):** Decoration chosen for looks instead of meaning.

**Measurement:** Would need semantic matching of an icon or emoji to its label, which is not possible with local heuristics. A candidate for the later opt-in or on-device-model phase.

**Known failure modes:** n/a

**Open questions:** Feasibility with an on-device model.
