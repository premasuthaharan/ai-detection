# C14 — Serif headline with sans-serif body

**Reported tell:** "Followed by a serif headline & sans serif paragraph" (S1b). Note S2 says the opposite default (safe sans-serif), so the tell may have shifted over time.

**Proposed mechanism (hypothesis):** Newer generator output copies an editorial look with a serif display face.

**Measurement:** 1 if the first h1 uses a serif family (generic serif or a known serif display font) and the main paragraph text is sans-serif, else 0.

**Known failure modes:** Editorial and publishing sites; many human-designed brands.

**Open questions:** Conflict with S2's sans-serif claim. Measure both.
