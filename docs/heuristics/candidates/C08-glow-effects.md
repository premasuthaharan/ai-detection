# C08 — Glow effects

**Reported tell:** "soft shadows and glow effects (supposed to create a sense of depth)" (S2).

**Proposed mechanism (hypothesis):** Generators add colored blurred shadows to suggest glow on dark themes.

**Measurement:** Count of elements with a saturated, non-gray box-shadow or text-shadow with blur of 12px or more.

**Known failure modes:** Dark gaming/crypto brands, focus rings and hover effects.

**Open questions:** Distinguishing decorative glow from focus styling.
