a small note on making things by hand

posted march 2019 · 4 min read

I keep coming back to the same handful of tools. A text editor, a terminal, and whatever the browser ships with. No build step, no framework, no dependency tree to keep patched.

This page is a good example. It's a few dozen lines of plain HTML and CSS, written directly, with no generator in between. It loads instantly and it will probably still work in another ten years, mostly because there's nothing in it that needs updating.

Some things I keep in mind when I write pages like this:

  • Prefer system fonts over anything that has to be downloaded.
  • Keep the color palette to two or three values, tops.
  • Don't animate anything that doesn't need to move.
  • If a table is the right tool, use a table.

None of this is a rule, exactly. It's just what tends to hold up. You can read more of what I write at the archive, or email me if something here was wrong.
